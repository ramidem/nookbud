2. **Verified records** — Every entry is immutable, timestamped, and server-verified
3. **Court-ready export** — PDF + evidence bundle with hash manifest, ready to share with any professional
4. **Acknowledgment system** — Know when your co-parent or guardian saw and responded to each entry
5. **For every family** — Solo parents, OFW families, grandparent guardians, blended families, open adoption
