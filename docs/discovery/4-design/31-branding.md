## Brand Identity

- **Name:** Nookbud
  - *Nook* — a small, sheltered corner. Safe. Private. Yours.
  - *Bud* — a child just beginning to grow. Small, tender, full of potential.
  - Together: *the safe corner where your child grows.*
- **Tagline:** *"Two homes. One childhood."*
- **Alt taglines:** "Co-parenting, clearly." / "The calm between two homes."
- **Domain:** nookbud.com (~$11/yr on Porkbun) — **not yet purchased**
- **Voice:** Calm, capable, warm. Non-judgmental. Never adversarial.
- **Personality:** The organized friend who keeps everything in order so you don't have to worry.

---

## Brand Values

| Value | In Practice |
|-------|-------------|
| **Clarity** | Every screen answers: *what do I need to know right now?* |
| **Calm** | No alarm colors unless action is actually needed |
| **Child-first** | Child is always the center — their schedule, records, world |
| **Trustworthy** | Records are tamper-resistant. What's logged, stays logged. |
| **Non-judgmental** | No assumptions about why the family is split |

## Logo

**Concept:** A bud (small sprout) nestled inside a nook (corner alcove). Geometric, minimal.

**Principles:**
- No faces, no cartoon characters
- Works in monochrome — flat color only (no gradients in primary mark)
- Scales from app icon (1024px) to favicon (16px)
- Readable at notification size (16px)

**Directions to explore (not yet decided):**
1. Rounded square with sprout cutout (most app-friendly)
2. Abstract nook corner with a bud emerging from it
3. Leaf folded into a corner — dual metaphor

**Status:** Logo SVGs exist in `assets/brand/logo/` (4 files — 2 concepts × color + mono). **Pick one direction** before proceeding to app icon production.
