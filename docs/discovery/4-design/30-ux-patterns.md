## Core UX Principles

### 1. Care-first, not conflict-first
The app should feel calm and organized, not adversarial. Every design decision should feel like: "Here's today. Here's what's next." — not "build your legal case."

### 2. Clarity over completeness
Show what matters now. Progressive disclosure for complexity.

### 3. Trust through transparency
Timestamps, version history, acknowledgment state — always visible. Users should feel the record is real and unalterable.

## Navigation Model
- Bottom tabs: Timeline, Children, Spaces, Export, Settings
- Space switcher accessible from top of Timeline
- All Spaces merged view as a toggle on Timeline
