## Conversion Funnel

```
Download → Onboard → Free Trial (14 days) → Convert → Retain → Expand
```

## Free-to-Paid Triggers
1. Trial expires (14-day time limit)
2. Need to invite a co-parent / Space member
3. Need to export a verified report
4. Need to upload files/attachments

## Free Tier Strategy
- No permanent free tier (see pricing.md rationale)
- 14-day trial, no credit card required
- Fee waiver program for low-income/hardship cases (follow AppClose model)
- 1 Space, solo use, no exports while in trial → convert to keep records

## Churn Prevention
- Records are the churn-preventer — leaving means losing your evidence trail
- Annual plan incentive: 30% discount vs monthly
- Low-friction billing: single-account covers entire Space
