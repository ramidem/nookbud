# Nookbud — Pricing Validation

> **Date:** 2026-03-29
> **For:** Nookbud market research
> **Decision:** $17.99/mo · $149.99/yr (adopted 2026-03-29)

---

## Executive Summary

Nookbud's adopted pricing of $17.99/mo ($149.99/yr) is validated as strategically sound. The price positions Nookbud in the premium-but-accessible tier, matching OurFamilyWizard Essentials' annual rate while covering the entire family (vs. OFW's per-parent billing). The effective household cost advantage is significant: $149.99/yr for Nookbud vs. $300–$600/yr for OFW.

Key findings:

1. **$17.99/mo is competitively positioned** — above the commodity tier ($6–$9), at the premium tier ($15–$25), but cheaper per household than the court-recognized incumbents
2. **The "one subscription covers the family" model is the strongest pricing differentiator** — Nookbud, WeParent, and 2Houses use this model; OFW, TalkingParents, and coParenter charge per parent
3. **Regional pricing for the Philippines may need adjustment** — the original ₱349/mo (~$6 USD) was set before the price increase to $17.99/mo. Recommend a separate PH/emerging market tier
4. **AppClose at $8.99/mo is the primary price competitor** — but lacks Nookbud's technical moats (SHA-256, Spaces, version-diffing)

## 3. Market-Specific Price Sensitivity

### United States (Primary Market)

- **WTP (willingness to pay):** High for legal-context users. Parents in active disputes don't price-shop.
- **Context:** One hour with a family law attorney costs $225–$325. Nookbud's annual price equals ~40 minutes of legal time.
- **$17.99/mo assessment:** Well-positioned. Below OFW, above AppClose, signals seriousness without being prohibitive.
- **Risk:** AppClose at $8.99/mo with Certified Electronic Business Records could attract price-conscious users who prioritize cost over features.

### Philippines (Expansion Market)

- **Original pricing:** ₱349/mo (~$6 USD) or ₱2,499/yr
- **Problem:** The $17.99/mo USD price is ~₱1,044/mo at current exchange rates — 3x the original PH pricing
- **Median monthly income:** ~₱18,000–₱25,000 for middle-income households
- **OFW remittance average:** $200–$500/mo sent home
- **Recommendation:** Maintain separate PH/emerging market pricing:

| PH Pricing Option | Monthly | Annual | USD Equivalent |
|-------------------|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Option A (original)** | ₱349/mo | ₱2,499/yr | ~$6/mo |
| **Option B (moderate increase)** | ₱499/mo | ₱3,999/yr | ~$8.60/mo |
| **Option C (aspirational)** | ₱699/mo | ₱4,999/yr | ~$12/mo |

**Recommended:** Option B (₱499/mo) — positions above commodity tier in PH market while remaining accessible to OFW families. The ~$8.60/mo USD equivalent is still below most US competitors.

### Australia

- **WTP:** High. Similar to US legal cost structure (~AUD 400–600/hr for family lawyers).
- **$17.99/mo assessment:** Very competitive. AUD equivalent (~$28/mo) feels natural for the market.
- **No adjustment needed** — global USD pricing works well here.

### Middle East (OFW Destination)

- **Users:** Primarily OFW workers sending money home. They earn in local currencies (SAR, AED, QAR).
- **WTP:** Moderate. OFWs are cost-conscious but motivated by family connection.
- **Recommendation:** Follow PH pricing for OFW-targeted marketing. The subscriber is typically the OFW parent abroad; the invited guardian back home joins free.

## 4. Freemium vs. Premium Analysis

### Current Market Approaches

| Model | Apps | Results |
|-------|------|---------|
| **No free tier** | OFW, 2Houses, coParenter, WeParent, Nookbud | Higher ARPU, lower acquisition |
| **Crippled free tier** | TalkingParents (web-only, ads) | High downloads, low conversion |
| **Generous free tier** | BestInterest (full messaging free) | Fast adoption, monetization unclear |
| **Extended trial** | AppClose (60 days) | Good trial-to-paid conversion expected |
| **Hardship waivers** | OFW, AppClose, WeParent, Custody X Change | Ethical positioning, PR value |

### Recommendation for Nookbud

**Short-term (launch):** 14-day free trial, no credit card. This is correct.

**Medium-term (6 months post-launch):** Consider adding a permanent free tier:
- 1 Space, 2 members max, no file uploads, no exports
- Purpose: Lower acquisition barrier, let users experience the product
- Critical: Free tier should NOT include tamper-proof guarantees (devalues the core product)

**Hardship program:** Follow AppClose's model — free accounts for DV survivors and financial hardship cases. This is both ethical and generates positive press.
