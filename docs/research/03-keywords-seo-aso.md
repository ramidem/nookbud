## Executive Summary

Keyword research for Nookbud spans three channels: App Store Optimization (ASO) for iOS/Android discoverability, website SEO for organic traffic, and paid search for targeted acquisition. The co-parenting keyword space is moderately competitive with strong commercial intent — users searching these terms are actively looking for solutions.

Nookbud has a unique positioning advantage: it can target keyword categories that no competitor occupies (OFW family apps, grandparent custody apps, shared care records). These long-tail categories have lower competition and higher conversion potential.
