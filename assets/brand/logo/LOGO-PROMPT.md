# Logo Generation Prompt — Nookbud

> Feed this prompt to an image-generating AI (ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc.)

---

```
Design a minimalist app icon for an app called Nookbud. No text, no letters, 
no wordmark anywhere in the image.

The mark: a small sprouting plant (two small leaves and a rounded bud tip) 
growing from the inner corner of an L-shaped nook. The nook is formed by two 
clean straight lines meeting at a right angle (like the corner of a room or 
alcove), with slightly rounded ends. The sprout grows upward from the corner 
junction, with one small leaf curving left and one curving right, and a small 
rounded bud at the top.

Style: flat, geometric, minimal. No gradients, no shadows, no outlines. 
Clean vector-style illustration. The mark must be recognizable at 32x32px — 
keep shapes bold and simple, avoid fine detail.

Colors:
- Background: rounded square, sage green #4A7C59
- Mark (nook lines + sprout + leaves): warm off-white #FAF8F4
- Bud tip only: soft amber #D4845A

The mark should be centered with generous padding from all edges.

Output: 4 variations, 1024x1024px each, rounded square background. 
Also provide one version on a transparent background (no rounded square) 
for web and favicon use. Absolutely no text or wordmark in any variation.
```

---

## Tips

- If it adds text: append *"absolutely no text, letters, or wordmark anywhere in the image"*
- Ask for a **transparent PNG** separately for favicon/web use
- If the sprout looks too complex at small sizes: *"simplify the mark, bolder shapes, fewer details"*
- If the nook angle is unclear: *"the L-shape should look like the inside corner of a room, not the outside"*
