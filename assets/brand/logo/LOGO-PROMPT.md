# Logo Generation Prompt — Nookbud

> Feed this prompt to an image-generating AI (ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc.)

---

```
Design a minimalist app icon for an app called Nookbud. No text, no letters, 
no wordmark anywhere in the image.

The mark: a single sprouting plant, large and centered, styled closely after 
the 🌱 seedling emoji. It has a short, slightly curved stem and two small 
rounded leaves — one curving to the left, one to the right — with a small 
rounded bud or shoot at the very top. The shapes are soft, organic, and 
rounded — not geometric or sharp.

The background is a rounded square in sage green #4A7C59. The green background 
itself represents the nook — no additional L-shape or corner lines needed.

The sprout should fill the icon generously — large enough to feel confident 
and readable, with comfortable but not excessive padding.

Style: flat, soft, organic. No gradients, no shadows, no outlines. 
Friendly and warm, not clinical or technical. The mark must be instantly 
readable at 32x32px — bold, simple shapes.

Colors:
- Background: rounded square, sage green #4A7C59
- Sprout (stem + leaves): warm off-white #FAF8F4
- Bud tip: soft amber #D4845A

Output: 4 variations, 1024x1024px each, rounded square background. 
Also provide one version on a transparent background (no rounded square) 
for web and favicon use. Absolutely no text or wordmark in any variation.
```

---

## Tips

- Reference emoji: 🌱 — match its roundness and proportions
- If it adds text: *"absolutely no text, letters, or wordmark anywhere"*
- If the sprout looks too small: *"make the sprout much larger, filling at least 60% of the icon"*
- If shapes look too sharp: *"softer, rounder, more organic — less geometric"*
- Ask for a **transparent PNG** separately for favicon/web use
