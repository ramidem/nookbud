# Spaces and members

A **space** is where the people who care for a child keep their shared records. It is private: only its members can see what is in it.

## One account, many spaces

You have one Nookbud account, and you can be a member of many spaces. For example, you might have one space for each household you share a child's care with. You switch between them without signing in again.

Each space has its own members and its own records. Nothing moves between spaces on its own.

## Members

A member is a person who belongs to a space. Members share the space's calendar and messages. The person who creates a space is its first **owner**.

## The owner

An owner looks after the space. An owner can:

- rename the space
- invite people to join it
- remove a member

## Invitations

An owner invites someone by email address. An invitation:

- works once
- expires
- can only be accepted by the person it was sent to, signed in with that email address

If an invitation reaches the wrong person, it does nothing. An owner can cancel an invitation before it is accepted.

## Leaving and removing

You can leave a space whenever you like, and an owner can remove a member. Either way, access ends straight away.

## Working alone

You do not need anyone else to use a space. Invite people when you are ready.
