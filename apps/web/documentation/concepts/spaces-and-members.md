# Spaces and members

A **space** is where the people who care for a child keep their shared records. It is private: only its members can see what is in it.

## One account, many spaces

You have one Nookbud account, and you can be a member of many spaces. For example, you might have one space for each household you share a child's care with. You switch between them without signing in again.

Each space has its own members and its own records. Nothing moves between spaces on its own.

## Members

A member is a person who belongs to a space. Everyone in a space can see who else is in it, and the children it is for. The person who creates a space is its first **owner**.

The shared calendar and messages are not available yet. They will live in the space when they are.

## The owner

An owner looks after the space. An owner can:

- rename the space
- add, change and remove children
- invite people to join it, as a member or as another owner
- make another member an owner, or an owner a member again
- remove a member
- delete the space

A space can have more than one owner, and it always has at least one. If you are the only owner and want to leave, make someone else an owner first.

## Invitations

An owner invites someone by email address. Nookbud makes a link, and the owner sends it however they like: a message, an email or in person. Nookbud does not email it yet.

An invitation:

- works once
- expires after 7 days
- can only be accepted by the person it was sent to, signed in with that email address

If an invitation reaches the wrong person, it does nothing. An owner can withdraw an invitation before it is accepted.

If the person has no account yet, the link lets them create one. Once they confirm their email, the invitation is waiting on their home page.

## Leaving and removing

You can leave a space whenever you like, and an owner can remove a member. Either way, access ends straight away. What you added stays in the space for the others.

## Deleting a space

An owner can delete a space. Nookbud asks them to type its name to confirm. Everyone loses access at once, and open invitations stop working.

## Working alone

You do not need anyone else to use a space. Invite people when you are ready.
