# The calendar

Every [space](spaces-and-members.md) has a shared calendar. Open the space, then **Calendar**. It shows a month at a time, in your own time zone.

## Events

Add school, health, and other events under **Add an event**. An event has a title, a time (or all day), a place if you like, and the person responsible.

Afterwards, the person who added the event or the person responsible can record what happened: **It happened** or **It was missed**, with a note. An event is a [record](records.md), so every change to it is kept in its history.

## The parenting schedule

An owner of the space can set up the parenting schedule: which home a child is in, day by day. Choose a pattern and the day it starts:

- **A week each**
- **Every other day**
- **2-2-3**, over two weeks
- **Day by day**, for any pattern up to 28 days

The pattern repeats, and the calendar shows whose day each day is, by name and color. Each change to the schedule is kept, with who changed it and when.

## Changing some days

Anyone in the space can ask for some days to be someone else's, for a trip or a swap. Choose the days, whose they would be, and why.

Someone else in the space then approves or declines. You cannot answer your own request, but you can withdraw it while it is waiting. An approved request changes those days on the calendar. Every request and answer stays listed, with who and when.

## Not available yet

- Connecting to other calendars.
