# Getting started

Nookbud is not open yet. This page shows what the first few minutes will look like, so you know what to expect. To hear when it opens, [join the waitlist](/) on the home page.

## 1. Create your account

You sign up with your email address and a password. There is no sign-in with Google, Apple or Facebook.

Nookbud sends you an email to confirm your address. Until you confirm it, you cannot open anything.

You also confirm that you are 18 or older. Nookbud is for adults who care for a child: parents, grandparents, guardians and other carers.

## 2. Tell us about your situation

Nookbud asks who shares responsibility for a child with you. You can choose a partner, an ex-partner, a grandparent, a guardian, another family member, or "I'm the sole caregiver right now". There is no wrong answer, and you can skip the question.

## 3. Create a space

A [space](concepts/spaces-and-members.md) is the private place where you and the people you care with keep your records. You give it a name, then add a child.

For a child, Nookbud asks for a name or nickname and a birth date. That is all.

## 4. Invite someone, or don't

You can invite another parent, a grandparent or a guardian by email. They get a link that only they can use, and it expires.

You can also start on your own. Using Nookbud alone is a normal way to begin, and you can invite people whenever you are ready.

## More than one household

You use one account for everything. If you share a child's care across more than one household, make a space for each arrangement and switch between them.
