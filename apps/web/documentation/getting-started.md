# Getting started

Nookbud is not open yet. Creating an account, spaces, invitations, records and the shared calendar are built. Messages, attachments and exports are not available yet. To hear when Nookbud opens, [join the waitlist](/) on the home page.

## 1. Create your account

You sign up with your email address and a password. There is no sign-in with Google, Apple or Facebook.

- Your password needs at least 12 characters. A few words you will remember work well.
- You confirm that you are 18 or older. Nookbud is for adults who care for a child: parents, grandparents, guardians and other carers.
- You accept the [terms of service](legal/terms.md) and the [privacy policy](legal/privacy.md).

Nookbud then sends you an email. Open the link in it to confirm your address. The link works once and expires after an hour. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder or ask for it again. Until you confirm your address, you cannot sign in.

## 2. Tell us about your situation

Nookbud asks your name and who shares responsibility for a child with you. You can choose a partner, an ex-partner, a grandparent, a guardian, another family member, or "I'm the sole caregiver right now". There is no wrong answer, and you can skip both questions.

## Signing in and out

- Sign in with your email and password. If either is wrong, Nookbud says so without saying which, so nobody can find out who has an account.
- You stay signed in while you use Nookbud. If you do not use it for 30 days, you are signed out and sign in again.
- **Sign out** is in the top bar. It ends your session on Nookbud's side, not only on your device.

## Your settings

Choose **Settings** in the top bar.

- **Profile:** your name, your time zone and your default currency. Your time zone starts from your device; dates and times are shown in it. You can also change your answer about who you care for a child with.
- **Security:** change your password, turn on [two-step sign-in](security-and-privacy.md#two-step-sign-in), and see [where you're signed in](security-and-privacy.md#where-youre-signed-in).

## Forgot your password

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email. If it has an account, Nookbud sends a link to choose a new password. The link works once and expires after an hour. When you save the new password, you are signed out on every device, and you sign in again with it.

## 3. Create a space

A [space](concepts/spaces-and-members.md) is the private place where you and the people you care with keep your records. On your home page, give it a name and choose **Create space**. Then add a child.

For a child, Nookbud asks for a name or nickname and, if you like, a birth date. That is all.

## 4. Invite someone, or don't

In the space, under **Invite someone**, enter the email of another parent, a grandparent or a guardian. Nookbud makes a link for them. Copy it and send it however you like. Only that email can use the link, once, within 7 days.

You can also start on your own. Using Nookbud alone is a normal way to begin, and you can invite people whenever you are ready.

## 5. Keep the record

In a space, the **Calendar** shows events and, once an owner sets up the [parenting schedule](concepts/calendar.md#the-parenting-schedule), whose day each day is. **Records** holds notes, agreements and expenses. See [records](concepts/records.md) for how changes are kept.

## More than one household

You use one account for everything. If you share a child's care across more than one household, make a space for each arrangement and switch between them.
