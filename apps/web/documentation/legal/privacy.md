# Privacy policy

> Version 2026-10-28. This is a draft. It will be reviewed by a lawyer under the Philippines Data Privacy Act before Nookbud opens, and may change. If it changes, we will ask you to accept the new version.

## What we keep

- **Your account:** your email address, your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), and when you accepted these policies and confirmed you are 18 or older.
- **Your profile:** your name, time zone and currency, if you add them.
- **What you add to a space:** for a child, a name or nickname and a birth date. We ask only for what a feature needs.
- **Security records:** when and from which device you sign in, and a record of security changes to your account (such as a changed password), so you can see your sessions and we can stop break-ins. These records hold no names or messages.

## Why we keep it

To run your account, to show your space to the people you invited, and to keep it secure. Nothing else.

## Who can see it

- Only the people in a space can see what is in it.
- We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising.
- We use a small number of companies to run Nookbud: hosting and storage (Supabase and Google Cloud, in Singapore) and sending email. They store or move your information for us and may not use it for anything else.

## How it is protected

Your information travels over an encrypted connection and is encrypted where it is stored. Passwords and messages are kept out of our logs. See [security and privacy](../security-and-privacy.md).

## What you can do

- See and correct your profile at any time.
- Ask for your account to be deleted, by writing to [hello@nookbud.com](mailto:hello@nookbud.com). Deleting it from inside Nookbud is coming.
- Ask us what we hold about you, or ask a question, at [hello@nookbud.com](mailto:hello@nookbud.com).

How long information is kept after an account is deleted is still being decided. It will be written here before Nookbud opens.

## Children

Nookbud is for adults. Children do not have accounts. Information about a child is added by the adults caring for them, and only the people in that child's space can see it.
