# Terms of service

> Version 2026-10-28. This is a draft. It will be reviewed by a lawyer and may change before Nookbud opens. If it changes, we will ask you to accept the new version.

These terms are an agreement between you and Nookbud. By creating an account, you accept them and the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Who can use Nookbud

- You must be 18 or older. Nookbud is for adults only. Children do not have accounts.
- One account is for one person. Keep your password to yourself.

## What Nookbud is

Nookbud is a shared record for adults raising a child across homes. It keeps schedules, notes and agreements in a [space](../concepts/spaces-and-members.md) that only the people you invite can see.

Nookbud is not legal, medical or financial advice. It does not take sides and does not decide anything for you.

## Your content

- What you add stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people in your space, and nothing else.
- Add only what you have the right to share.
- Records are kept with their history. People in your space can see what changed and when.

## Using it fairly

Do not use Nookbud to harass, threaten or watch someone, to break the law, or to break into other people's accounts or spaces. We may suspend an account that does.

## Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in **Settings**. Anything you shared in a space stays there for the others, shown as from a "Former member". The [privacy policy](privacy.md) says what happens to your information.

## Changes and availability

Nookbud is in development. Features may change, and the service may be unavailable at times. We will tell you before these terms change.
