# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Your sign-in to Nookbud. One account can belong to many spaces. |
| Acknowledgment | A member's reply to an entry that shows they have seen it. See [Records](concepts/records.md). |
| Child | A child whose care a space records. Children do not have accounts. |
| Dispute | A member's challenge to an entry, with a reason and, if they have it, evidence. |
| Entry | One record of something that happened: an expense, an event, an agreement, a structured communication or an upload. |
| File | A photo, receipt or document attached to an entry. Nookbud keeps a fingerprint of it so it cannot be altered unseen. |
| Invitation | A single-use link, tied to one email address, that adds someone to a space. It expires. |
| Member | A person who belongs to a space. |
| Owner | A member who looks after a space: they rename it, invite people and remove members. |
| Space | The private place where the people who care for a child keep their shared records. See [Spaces and members](concepts/spaces-and-members.md). |
| Two-step sign-in | An extra code from an authenticator app, asked for when you sign in. It is optional. |
