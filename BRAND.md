### Logo Concept

A bud (small sprout) nestled inside a nook (corner alcove). Geometric, minimal. One color works. Readable at 16px notification size.

```
  ╭──╮
  │ 🌱 │
  ╰───╯
```

**Principles:**
- No faces, no cartoon characters
- Works in monochrome (single color)
- Scales from app icon (1024px) to favicon (16px)
- No gradients in primary mark — flat color only

**Icon shape options to explore:**
1. Rounded square with sprout cutout (most app-friendly)
2. Abstract nook corner with a bud emerging from it
3. Leaf folded into a corner — dual metaphor

## ✍️ Voice & Tone

**Personality:** Calm, capable, present. Like a dependable friend who's also competent.

**Write like this:**
- Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- Never dramatic: ~~"We know this is a tough time"~~
- Just present and useful: *"Here's today. Here's what's next."*
- Address the parent directly: "You", not "Parents" or "Users"

**Never:**
- Preachy or judgmental ("for the sake of your children")
- Over-warm or saccharine ("we're here for your journey!")
- Cold or clinical ("co-parenting event logged successfully")

**Examples:**

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---------|-------|
| "Event successfully added to the co-parenting timeline." | "Added. Both parents can see it." |
| "We know co-parenting can be challenging." | "Nookbud keeps things clear when things get complicated." |
| "Your child's wellbeing is our top priority." | "Everything in one place — for them." |

## 📱 App Experience Principles

**1. The child is always the center**
Dashboard leads with the child's view — their schedule, their needs, what's happening in their world. Not the parents' conflict.

**2. Breathing room**
White space is intentional. Stressed parents need visual calm, not information density.

**3. Action-clear**
Every screen has one obvious next action. No decision paralysis.

**4. Neutral ground**
The app never takes sides. Both parents see the same truth.

**5. Ready when needed**
Records are always export-ready. Court-ready format baked in — but never mentioned unless needed.
