---

> Generated: 2026-03-28  
> Goal: Relatable, family/children-hinting name. Max 3 syllables. Domain available. Open to any language.  
> Existing competitors to avoid sounding like: OurFamilyWizard, TalkingParents, 2Houses, Coparently, AppClose, WeParent, Custody X Change

---

## 🧭 Naming Directions Explored

1. **Filipino words** — care, nurture, protection, family
2. **Animal co-parents** — species known for cooperative parenting (penguins, seahorses, wolves, geese, cranes, gibbons, otters, flamingos, swans)
3. **Abstract warmth** — nest, harbor, tether, kindred, fledgling
4. **Cross-language** — Japanese (kizuna), Spanish (nido), Latin (amara)
5. **Coined/blended** — brand-friendly invented words

## 🗺️ Domain Availability Key

- ✅ **LIKELY FREE** — DNS returns NXDOMAIN (unregistered)
- ❌ **TAKEN** — DNS resolves (registered/parked/active)
- ❓ **UNCLEAR** — Inconclusive (check manually on Namecheap/GoDaddy)

> Note: Always verify on a registrar before purchasing. DNS checks are ~95% reliable.
